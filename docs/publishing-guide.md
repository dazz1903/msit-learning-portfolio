# Publishing Coursework and Evidence

This is a public repository: files, pull requests, and commit history may be copied. These are portfolio handling rules, not a statement of MSIT or Codio's actual publication policy.

## Confirm what may be shared

Check the applicable course instructions and provider rules for each item. If the scope is unclear, obtain clarification before publishing the material. Keep the permission record privately and note only the permitted scope here.

Completion, grading, or access through Codio does not itself establish permission to redistribute an assessment.

## Choose a suitable public version

- Prefer an original case study explaining your approach and learning.
- Publish coursework code only within the permitted scope. Otherwise keep it in a separate private location.
- Do not publish assessment prompts, solution keys, hidden tests, proprietary teaching materials, other students' work, or restricted datasets.
- Label independent extensions and starter code accurately, and credit sources and collaborators.
- Follow course rules for collaboration, external tools, and AI assistance.

## Handle grades and instructor comments faithfully

Use actual records only. Preserve the original score, denominator or grade scale, evaluation date, and whether the result was assigned by an instructor or generated automatically. Do not silently convert grades.

Publish quotations only when sharing is permitted; preserve their wording and context. Label summaries, omissions, and translations. Do not imply endorsement. Keep instructor identities and contact information private unless their publication is approved.

## Remove private and sensitive material

Check source code, configuration, logs, screenshots, filenames, document metadata, and links for credentials, access tokens, student IDs, email addresses, personal information, private platform URLs, and unrelated records. Use synthetic data and example configuration.

Keep originals, raw exports, graded submissions awaiting review, and permission correspondence outside this repository. The ignored `private/` and `raw-exports/` paths are convenience safeguards only: ignored files can still be force-added, and ignore rules do not remove tracked files or history.

## Review before publishing

Inspect the complete proposed diff and every final artifact, including rendered screenshots. Confirm permissions, redaction, provenance, attribution, and working public links. Do not attach an original private record to a public pull request for review.

If sensitive material is published accidentally, remove public access promptly where possible. Revoke exposed credentials, and address repository history and retained copies as appropriate; deleting a file in a later commit does not erase earlier versions.

## Licensing

No license has been selected in this setup. Do not apply a blanket open-source license to course materials or third-party content without the necessary rights. Choose licenses deliberately for your own publishable work and retain third-party notices.
